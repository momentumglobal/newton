Frequently Asked Questions

Who can open My Momentum?
Everyone who can sign in to Newton. It is your own area, so each tab only appears when it applies to you. My Momentum is part of the Newton desktop site and is not in the mobile app.
Why is a tab missing?
My History needs your work email to match one employee record. My Details needs the same (Admin and People Ops see it without one). My Scorecard appears when Newton has activity logged under your name. My Development appears when Development & Performance is switched on for you. My Onboarding appears when onboarding is switched on for you or you have a plan of your own. Buddying appears when you are the buddy on a joiner’s active plan. My Team appears when people report to you, or when you are Admin, Leadership or People Ops. If a tab you expect is missing, ask People Ops.
I see "Not linked to an employee record"
Your sign-in is not matched to a People record yet. Ask People Ops to add your work email to your record. "More than one employee record matches" means your work email is on two records, and People Ops need to fix it.
Who can see my details?
The emergency contact, personal contact details and home address on My Details can be seen by you, Admin and People Ops. Nobody else can, including Leadership, and they are never shown in Newton for Claude, the Command Bar, exports or downloads.
Can someone else enter my details for me?
Admin and People Ops can add or correct details on your behalf. Every change is stamped with who made it and when, and your page says when it was done for you.
Why is My Details closed when I view Newton as someone else (Ghost Mode)?
My Details shows real personal data, so it stays closed while you view Newton as another person.
Who can see my Development & Performance records?
You, your line manager, your CSD, and Admin and People Ops. Leadership does not see them by default. Your line manager is the person above you on the Org Chart.
Why is My Development or the Development view of My Team missing?
They only appear when Development & Performance is switched on for you. While it is being piloted, that means the named pilot people plus their line managers and CSDs. Once it is On, it means everyone in the Delivery team plus their line managers and CSDs. My Development also needs your work email to be linked to your People record, and the Development view of My Team needs people to report to you in Newton. If it looks wrong, ask People Ops.
Can I change my prep after the meeting has happened?
You can keep editing your prep until your line manager marks the meeting as held. After that it is read-only. Goals and actions can still be updated afterwards from the Goals & actions tab, although the wording of anything agreed in a signed meeting is locked.
What happens to my records if my line manager changes?
Your new line manager and CSD see your whole history. Your previous line manager and CSD keep read-only access to the records from when they managed you.
Why haven't I had any PDP reminders?
Reminders only start once Development & Performance is On (people in the pilot get none), and never for meetings or actions due before the "Reminders from" date that an Admin sets. They appear as notifications on the Newton bell and never contain the content of your meetings.
Who can see my onboarding progress?
You, your line manager and your CSD. Your buddy sees the steps that are theirs, and the rest of your plan for context. Admin can see every plan. Leadership cannot see onboarding progress.
Why can’t I tick an onboarding step?
You can tick the steps you own. A step owned by your line manager, buddy, CSD, People Ops or Operations / IT is ticked by them, and your line manager or CSD can tick any step for you. The intake role-play and the Day-30 review are signed off by your line manager in My Team, not ticked. A plan that is complete is read-only.
Why haven’t I had an onboarding reminder?
Reminders only go out while onboarding is Pilot or On, only for steps you own that are due today or overdue, and only for steps with a due date. Operations / IT steps remind nobody. A reminder appears the next time you open My Momentum, not before.
What happens if a joiner’s start date changes?
Every due date moves with it. Due dates are always worked out from the start date, so the CSD only needs to correct the start date on the employee record. There is no pause.
Are Bi-Annual scores really blind?
Yes. You score yourself and your line manager scores you without seeing each other's scores. Nobody, including Admin and People Ops, sees either set until both sides have submitted and the scores are revealed.

Getting there, and which tabs you see

My Momentum is your personal area in Newton: your history at Momentum, your own details, your scorecard, your development and, if people report to you, your team. There are four ways in.

  • Home: the greeting at the top of the Newton home screen. Click Step into My Momentum.
  • The sidebar: in every module, click your name at the top of the sidebar. The notification bell next to it still opens your notifications.
  • The Command Bar (Ctrl+K): search for My Momentum, My Scorecard, Team Scorecards, My Development, My Onboarding or My Team.
  • The module menu: My Momentum is listed there for every role.

The tabs

The sidebar on the left lists only the tabs that apply to you, always in this order, and you land on the first one: My History, My Details, My Scorecard, My Development, My Onboarding, Buddying, My Team. The FAQ above says when each one appears.

Your employee record

Newton finds you from your work email, which has to match one record in People. If it does not, My Momentum shows "Not linked to an employee record" instead of the tabs, and tells you to ask People Ops.

Admin and People Ops usually have no employee record of their own. They still get My Details (to look after other people's), My Team and, where Development & Performance applies, the PDP pages.

My History

Everyone with an employee record

Your landing tab. It shows your time at Momentum and the projects you have worked on.

The tiles

@ Momentum since is the start date on your People record (the tile is left out if no start date has been recorded). Projects completed counts the projects in your record below that are completed. Total hires counts the placements credited to you in Newton, across all time. It is the same count as the Hires figure on your scorecard, and it shows 0 if you have none.

Your project record

One line per project: Project, Type, Start, End (Ongoing when there is no end date) and Status. The status is Current, Upcoming or Completed. A line is Completed when your own end date has passed or the project itself has been completed.

Current projects come first, then Upcoming, then Completed with the most recent first. Where an assignment was extended by signing a new one straight after the old one, the two show as a single line. A gap of a day or more starts a new line. Bench periods, forecast assignments and old assignments that Newton has no project for are not listed.

The project name opens the Project page if your role can open it. Otherwise it shows as plain text.

My Details

Employee (your own) Admin People Ops

The details People Ops need if something goes wrong: who to call in an emergency, how to reach you outside work and where you live. Only you, Admin and People Ops can see them.

What you enter

Three cards, each saved with Save:

  • Emergency contact: one primary contact. Name, relationship and phone are needed once you fill any of the card in. An alternative phone is optional.
  • Personal contact: your personal mobile and personal email.
  • Home address: address line 1, town / city and country are needed once you fill any of the card in. Address line 2 and postcode are optional.

Phone numbers can use digits, spaces, + ( ) - and full stops. Fields with a problem are marked when you save, and nothing is saved until they are fixed. If you leave the tab with unsaved changes, Newton asks first. The page shows when your details were last updated.

Adding or correcting details for someone else

Admin and People Ops see Whose details at the top. Type a name to search, choose the person and edit their card. The page says you are editing that person's details, the change is stamped with your name and the time, and the employee's own page says it was updated on their behalf. The same details are shown (read-only, with an Edit details button) on the employee record page in People.

Manage (Admin and People Ops)

The Manage card keeps the details list healthy. Press Check to run the checks. They only show names, never the details themselves:

  • People with no row, with the Create button (it shows the count, for example "Create 3 rows") to add the missing ones.
  • People with no work email (their details cannot be locked to them yet).
  • Row email needs setting to the work email, with the Fix button (it shows the count, for example "Fix 3 emails").
  • Rows not locked after the grace period: the automatic lock has not run. Check the flow's run history in Power Automate.
  • People with more than one row.
  • Leavers still holding details, with Clear contents to blank a leaver's details. The row stays, and earlier versions remain in SharePoint's version history until a site admin removes them.
Each person's row is locked to them by an automatic flow shortly after it is created. Until then only Admin and People Ops can read it. A person with no work email on their People record cannot be locked yet, so they cannot see their details until one is added.

My Scorecard

Anyone with activity logged under their name

Your own activity analytics and performance benchmarking: a card showing your pipeline activity and how well it converts through the funnel. The tab appears when Newton has activity logged under your name, which in practice means Talent Partners, Delivery Managers and Internal TA staff.

Choosing a period

Use Period to switch between Rolling quarter (the last 13 weeks), Calendar YTD (this calendar year to date) and All time. The card says which window it is showing. Hires follows the period.

What the scorecard shows

The card shows your pipeline activity and funnel conversion metrics across all your roles. This gives a consistent, comparable picture of performance: how much activity is being generated, and how effectively it is converting through the funnel.

Benchmarking

Conversion rates are compared against learned benchmarks: what comparable roles actually achieve, learned from the last 52 weeks of Weekly Activity. The benchmark is the same whichever period you pick, so a red, amber or green means the same thing on every period. Your benchmark is weighted by the functions and locations you work in, so someone on hard markets is compared with those markets, and your own activity is left out so you are never benchmarked against yourself. Where there is little data, the benchmark leans toward Newton's company targets (CONFIG.ANALYTICS_BENCHMARKS), and it never drops below 80% of the target, so a weak market can't hide a real gap. A learned benchmark can also sit above target: in a strong market, hitting the target may still show amber. Hover over a rate to see the benchmark used. Contact your Newton Admin if the targets or learning settings (CONFIG.LEARNED_BENCHMARKS) need adjusting.

Hires counts the placements logged under your name in the period (by Offer Accepted date), the same figure the Placements table gives for you. Offer success is worked out from the offers and hires Talent Partners record in Weekly Activity, so on one card the two can differ.

Detail under your own scorecard

Under your card (not on Team Scorecards) three sections follow. Weekly trend and Hires follow the Period choice. Flagged roles always shows today's open roles. Weekly trend draws one small chart per metric (Outreach conversion, Submission conversion, Interview-to-offer, Offer success, Hires and Average time to hire), one point per week. A dashed line marks your 52-week norm; Interview-to-offer uses the 7:1 limit and Hires has no line. Weeks with no data are skipped and the line joins the weeks that have it. For Interview-to-offer and Average time to hire, lower is better.

Flagged roles lists the open roles behind the card's flagged count, with the reason (stuck in stage, low conversion, or both), and separately the roles behind the 45-day pace count, which is for information only. Role names open the Role page for Admin, Delivery Managers and Talent Partners; for Leadership and People Ops they show as plain text. The Hires section lists the placements behind your Hires number: candidate, role, project and the Offer Accepted date.

Scorecard data is calculated from Weekly Activity records, except Hires and Average time to hire, which come from Placements. Ensure activity is being logged consistently each week for the funnel metrics to be accurate.

Internal TA staff, who log Momentum's own hiring on the Momentum Global (Internal) project, get a card built from that work. It is compared against client benchmarks, and internal activity never feeds anyone else's benchmark.

My Development

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

Your own page: your year of meetings, your prep, the agreed record of each meeting, and your goals and actions. It appears as a tab in My Momentum once PDP is live for you and your work email is linked to your People record.

My year

The top of the page shows your name, level, line manager and CSD, with counts of meetings done, due soon and overdue, and a Year selector. Under it, My year shows one card per meeting with its due date and a status: Upcoming, Due soon, Overdue, Done or Skipped (with the reason). A line on the card says where it is up to, for example "Prep open", "Prep ready", "Held · your sign-off is needed" or "Signed off". Click a card to open the meeting.

Preparing

The Annual, Bi-Annual and Quarterly meetings have prep questions for you to answer beforehand. Prep opens on the first day of the month the meeting is due. For a Quarterly it opens two days before the meeting date your line manager sets, and for a joiner's goal setting it opens 30 days before it is due. Use Save draft as you go and Mark prep ready when you are done. Your answers are private to you, your line manager and your CSD, and you can keep editing until the meeting is marked as held.

The Monthly Development Check-In needs no prep. The meeting shows "No prep needed for this meeting" and has no prep buttons. The Quarterly meeting has a question about performance against KPIs: answer it in your own words, and use Open my Scorecard to open the My Scorecard tab, if you have one.

After the meeting

Once your line manager marks the meeting as held, you can read the agreed record: the notes, the goals and the actions, plus your manager's prep. Click Sign off this record to sign. Your line manager signs as well, and the meeting becomes Signed off once you have both signed.

The Goals & actions tab beside My year shows your goal tracker (see Goals & actions below).

My Team

Admin Leadership People Ops Line managers and CSDs

For anyone who manages people. Your team is the people who report to you on the Org Chart (the same line the Development & Performance pages use). A Delivery Manager is listed as the line manager of their Talent Partners, so they see them here. My Team has up to two views, shown as tabs: Development and Team Scorecards.

Who sees which view

Team Scorecards is for anyone with people reporting to them, and for Admin, Leadership and People Ops. It works whether or not Development & Performance is switched on. Development appears when Development & Performance applies to you and people report to you in Newton. Admin and People Ops always see it. A Leadership member sees Development only if people report to them, so otherwise they get Team Scorecards alone.

The View filter

Both views share the same View choice: Whole tree (a CSD sees everyone beneath them), Direct reports and All staff (Admin, Leadership and People Ops). Admin sees everyone.

Team Scorecards

One card per person in the view, each built exactly like My Scorecard (see above) and following the same Period choice, A to Z by name. People who have left, placeholders and people with no work email are not shown. If nobody is in the view, the tab says "No scorecards to show". The detail sections that sit under your own card (weekly trend, flagged roles, hires) are not shown here.

Development

The team table and meetings for the people who report to you. A CSD sees everyone beneath them. Admin and People Ops always see it.

The team table

The table lists each person, their line manager, their next meeting and what needs attention, plus how many meetings they have done. Counts across the top show how many meetings are overdue, due soon, prep ready, waiting for a signature, and how many people have no meetings yet. People with something overdue or waiting for you are listed first.

Use View to switch between Whole tree, Direct reports and All staff. All staff is for Admin and People Ops and is read-only except for the people they manage themselves. Use Year to look at other years. For a Quarterly meeting you can set the meeting date straight from the table.

One person's year

Click a name to see that person's year. The Meetings tab lists their meetings and the Goals & actions tab shows their tracker.

Running a meeting

Open a meeting to see the employee's prep, and the commitments from the previous held meeting that are still open, so nothing is dropped. Write your own prep and the discussion notes, record the goals and actions agreed, and set the meeting date. If something is missing, a box titled Before you can mark this as held lists it. When you are ready, click Mark as held, then Sign off as manager.

You can keep editing a held meeting until the first signature (yours or the employee's). After that the record is locked.

What you can change depends on whether you are named on that person's record, not on your Newton role. Only the person's own line manager or CSD can run their meeting, even though CSDs have the Admin role. Anyone else sees the record read-only.

My Onboarding

Talent Partner Joiner

My Onboarding is your 30-day plan when you join as a Talent Partner. It lists every step with who owns it and when it is due, and ends with a Day-30 review by your line manager. Days 31 to 90 are covered by your monthly Development & Performance check-ins.

The sub-tabs

Onboarding Plan is your own plan and only appears if you have one. Processes & Rituals, Expectations & SLAs and Learning Library are reference pages for everyone (see below).

Your plan

The top of the page shows your start date, line manager and buddy, how many steps are done, how many are overdue and your next step. Steps are grouped by phase. Each one shows its owner, its due day (the start day reads Day 1 (start day), so week 1 ends on Day 5) and its due date. Guided steps are sessions you must complete. Self-guided steps are material you work through yourself. Open takes you to the deck or file, which opens in your browser.

Steps that come before your start day appear when you start. The Library at the bottom is optional self-guided material: filter it by level (All levels, Foundation, Experienced), tick items once you have used them. Library items have no due date and do not count towards your progress. The same items are always in the Learning Library tab; the link next to Browse the Library takes you there.

Who ticks a step

You tick the steps you own. Other steps are ticked by their owner, and your line manager and CSD can tick any step for you; Newton records who ticked each one. While there is no People Ops person at Momentum, People Ops steps are done by your CSD and show as People Ops — CSD for now. Two steps, the intake role-play and the Day-30 review, are signed off by your line manager in My Team and are not ticked by hand.

While onboarding is switched Off, an Admin can preview a plan. A preview is read-only.

Buddying

Buddy

If you are named as a joiner’s buddy you get a Buddying tab. The tab label shows how many of your steps are still open, for example Buddying (3). For each joiner, Your actions comes first: the steps owned by the buddy, which you can tick. Below it, Rest of the joiner’s plan is there for context only and cannot be ticked.

The tab shows only while the joiner’s plan is active. If you are also a joiner yourself you get both Buddying and My Onboarding, and My Onboarding shows only your own plan.

Processes & Rituals, Expectations & SLAs, and Learning Library

Everyone

Processes & Rituals shows how work is done at Momentum: the individual rituals and the Newton data rituals (the weekly activity log and regular role status updates) as step-by-step cards, the project and team rituals as a list, and a pointer to the Role Checklist.

Expectations & SLAs sets out the standards for candidates and stakeholders, the quality bar and the delivery KPIs. The SLA defaults and scorecard benchmarks are read live from Newton, so they are always current. Not set means there is no company default for that metric, and a project can carry its own target instead.

Learning Library lists the self-guided learning material for everyone. Filter it by level (All levels, Foundation, Experienced); Open takes you to the deck or file, and Browse the Library opens the whole Library folder. There is nothing to tick here: joiners tick these items in their Onboarding Plan.

Once onboarding is On, these pages are open to everyone who can open My Momentum. While it is in Pilot they show only to the pilot people and anyone involved in a plan.

My Team: Onboarding

Admin Line managers and CSDs

For the line manager and CSD of a joiner. My Team gets an Onboarding view showing your joiners’ plans. Admin sees every plan.

What you see

Three tiles: Active joiners, Overdue steps and Awaiting sign-off. My actions lists the steps you own, with the role-play and Day-30 sign-offs placed at their step’s due position and the countersign last. With two or more active joiners, the Joiner menu narrows My actions and the joiner cards to one person; the tiles stay team-wide. Each card in Joiners shows progress, overdue steps and the next step, and Show details lists the open steps by owner. Finished plans move to Completed.

Drafting the welcome email

The pre-start step Welcome email and Week 1 agenda sent; buddy named has a Draft welcome email button, in My actions and on the joiner’s card, for whoever can tick it. It shows a ready-made, Momentum-branded email: the joiner’s first-week agenda with a card for each day (every step due on Days 1 to 5, with dates), their buddy’s name and work email, and their line manager. Open in Outlook starts a new mail with the subject filled in and copies the email: click into the body and paste. Copy email copies it on its own, to paste anywhere. Add the joiner’s email address, check it reads right and send it.

Newton doesn’t send the email or tick the step. Tick it yourself once the email has gone. Set the buddy on the employee record first, or the email says a buddy will be introduced.

Signing off

The Sign-offs panel has three rows. The line manager records Intake role-play (M05) with Sign off once the joiner has passed it, then Day-30 review: line manager. The Day-30 sign-off is available when every required step is ticked and the role-play is signed off. Withdraw undoes a sign-off; the role-play can only be withdrawn after the Day-30 sign-off has been withdrawn. The CSD then uses Countersign and close plan, available only after the line manager’s Day-30 sign-off. Closing a plan cannot be undone and makes it read-only.

The panel also shows whether the joiner’s Development & Performance goal-setting meeting is due, held or signed off. It is for information and never blocks a sign-off.

Only the joiner’s own line manager can sign off the role-play and the Day-30 review, and only their CSD can countersign. Anyone else sees the reason beside the button.

Onboarding reminders

Owners of onboarding steps get reminders on the notification bell: one note a day for steps due today and one for overdue steps, each just a count, for example “You have 3 onboarding steps due today.” It opens your plan, My Team: Onboarding or Buddying, depending on whose step it is. Reminders appear the next time you open My Momentum. They go out while onboarding is Pilot or On, and never for Operations / IT steps, steps with no due date, or the CSD countersign.

Starting and running a plan

Admin CSD

Off, Pilot and On

In Reporting → Config Panel → Onboarding, an Admin chooses how far onboarding reaches. Off: only Admin can preview. Pilot: the named pilot joiners, their line managers and CSDs, and anyone with a role on a plan. On: every Talent Partner (TP, STP, PTP) and anyone with a role on a plan. Leadership does not see onboarding progress.

Starting a plan

Open the new joiner’s employee record in People and click Start onboarding on the Onboarding card, choosing a buddy if you have one (you can set or change it later). The joiner needs a unique work email and a “Reports to”, and a start date that is today, in the future or no more than 14 days ago. If the button is missing, the card says why. Cancel plan hides the plan from the joiner, line manager and buddy but keeps its ticks, and Restart plan brings it back. There is no pause: if a start is delayed, correct the start date and every due date follows.

Changing the steps

In Reporting → Config Panel → Checklists → Onboarding each step has an owner, a due day counted from the start day (0 is Day 1), a mode (Guided or Self-guided), an optional level and a link to its content. Changing a due day moves that step on every live plan.

Checking the data

Data Health in the Admin module has an Onboarding plans section with eight read-only checks (for example a plan whose person has no line manager). They should all read zero.

Development & Performance: how it works

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

Development & Performance (PDP) is where Newton keeps the four development conversations between a person and their line manager: the meeting notes, the goals agreed and the actions that follow. It starts with the Delivery team (levels TP, STP, PTP and SDM). CSDs take part as reviewers.

The four meetings

Annual Development & Goal Setting in January. Bi-Annual Performance & Progression Review in July. Quarterly Review in April and October. Monthly Development Check-In in every other month. That makes twelve meetings a year. Each one is due on the last day of its month.

Newton tracks what is due, due soon and overdue. It does not send calendar invites: line managers book the meetings in Outlook themselves.

Who sees what

Each person's records are visible to the person, their line manager, their CSD, and Admin and People Ops. Leadership does not see them by default. The employee can complete their own prep, track their goals and actions, and sign off each meeting.

Off, Pilot and On

An Admin controls how far PDP reaches. Off: nobody sees it except Admin and People Ops, who can preview it. Pilot: only the named pilot people and their line managers and CSDs see it, and nobody is sent reminders. On: everyone in scope and their reviewers see it, and reminders start. Someone removed from the pilot keeps seeing their existing records. Switching to Off hides PDP from everyone except Admin and People Ops, even people who already have records.

Joiners, existing staff and promotions

A new joiner's first meeting is an Annual-format goal setting, due 30 days after they start. Any calendar meeting due within three weeks after that is skipped. The July Bi-Annual is only scored if they started on or before 31 March.

Someone who already works at Momentum and is brought into PDP part-way through the year follows the normal calendar. Meetings whose month has already passed are created as Skipped with the reason "Before PDP start".

If someone is promoted, their Bi-Annual is scored against the new level once they have been in it for three months.

Admin and People Ops can preview PDP even when it is Off or when they are not in the pilot. A preview is read-only: nothing can be saved or signed.

Goals & actions

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

The Goals & actions tab appears on My Development and on a person's page in the Development view of My Team. It is one live view of the year's goals and actions, and you can update it between meetings without opening a meeting.

Goals

Goals cascade: Annual goals, then H2 goals for the second half of the year, then Quarterly goals. A goal can be linked to a parent goal above it. Each goal has a category (Business, Development, Behaviour or Career), and can record why it matters, how success will be measured, the support needed and a target date.

Every goal has a RAG (Red, Amber or Green) and a status (Open, Achieved or Dropped). A goal's rolled-up RAG shows the worst RAG among its open goals beneath it, so you can see where the pressure is without opening each one. There is a limit on how many open goals one person can hold at each level in a year.

Actions

Each action has an owner (the employee or the manager) and a due date, and is ticked off when it is done. How many actions a meeting can record is set in the meeting template.

Who can edit

The employee, their line manager and their CSD can add and edit goals and actions. Once a meeting is signed, the wording of the goals and actions agreed in it is locked. RAG, status and the done tick can always be changed. A goal you no longer want is marked as Dropped rather than deleted. When the year's cycle is closed, everything is read-only.

Bi-Annual scoring

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

The July meeting includes a scoring block. The employee and the line manager each score six pillars from 1 to 3 against the descriptors for the employee's level. There is no reward or salary recommendation in this review.

Step by step

  • Employee self-score. In My Development, open the Bi-Annual and score each pillar with your evidence. Use Save scores as draft until you are ready to submit.
  • Manager score. The line manager (or CSD) scores the same pillars from the Development view of My Team, with a rationale for each. Scoring is blind: neither side can see the other's scores yet.
  • Reveal. Once both sides have submitted, Newton reveals each side's scores to the other. This takes a few minutes and the block shows it is unlocking. Until then nobody else, including Admin and People Ops, can see either set.
  • Agreeing scores in the meeting. The manager records an agreed score per pillar. If the two scores are the same, that is the agreed score. If they are one point apart, the manager decides: the agreed score must be one of the two, with a reason. If they are two points apart, the pillar goes to the calibration panel.
  • Overall score and band. The overall score is the average of the six pillars to one decimal place: 1.0 to 1.5 is Below, 1.6 to 2.4 is Meets and 2.5 to 3.0 is Exceeding. A total of exactly 1.5 or 2.5 sits on a boundary: the manager agrees which of the two bands it belongs in, or sends it to the panel.
  • Progression readiness. The manager chooses Ready now, Ready in 6–12 months or Not yet ready, with a note.
  • Manager track (Level 4 only). Newton suggests a manager-track flag when the agreed score is 3 on Support, Curious and Adapt. The manager confirms it, with a reason, or leaves it.

A Bi-Annual can only be marked as held once both sides have submitted, the scores are revealed, every pillar is agreed or sent to the panel, any boundary total is settled and readiness is chosen. Signing off is allowed while a pillar is still with the panel.

Scoring for the PTP level stays switched off until the descriptors for that level have been written in PDP Setup. Until then the scoring block shows that there are no descriptors for the level.

Old links

These pages used to sit in the People module. Bookmarks and links in old emails still work and take you to the new place after you sign in:

  • Scorecards opens My Scorecard. If you have no scorecard of your own (for example a manager with no logged activity) it opens Team Scorecards instead.
  • My Development opens the My Development tab.
  • Team Development opens My Team.

PDP reminders on the Newton bell open the same new places. PDP Setup has not moved: it is still in the People module, and is described in the People guide.
