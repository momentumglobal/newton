Frequently Asked Questions

Who can access the People module?
Admin, Leadership and People Ops have full access to all pages. Sales & Marketing and Staff see the Org Chart only (read-only) and land there. Delivery Managers and Talent Partners can open Scorecards (a Delivery Manager sees the Talent Partners on their projects; a Talent Partner sees their own card only) and the Org Chart (read-only). They land on Scorecards when they open the module. Delivery Managers and Talent Partners also see My Development, and Team Development if people report to them, when Development & Performance applies to them.
How does utilisation get calculated?
Utilisation is person-based: for each person (excluding CSD level), it counts the days they were on at least one billed assignment, divided by the days they were on any assignment. Each person counts once per day, so someone on one billed and two unbilled customers at the same time is 100% utilised. Days are pro-rated within each month.
Why do the dashboard figures only show up to today?
Actual utilisation and revenue are counted up to the end of the current month. Even if an assignment runs further into the future, later months are not included in the actual figures. The dotted forecast line and the Command Centre forecast look ahead separately, using assignments already scheduled.
Why does an employee appear in multiple customer groups on the Deployment Timeline?
An employee can have assignments with more than one customer. Each assignment is grouped under its own customer, so the same employee may appear in multiple sections of the timeline if they have worked across customers during the selected year.
What does "Estimated Revenue" mean?
Revenue figures are calculated from monthly bill rates and assignment dates. They are estimates based on signed assignment data — actual invoiced amounts may differ slightly. Supplier Invoice amounts are tracked separately in the Supplier Invoices page.
How often does the data update?
Newton reads directly from SharePoint Lists via the Microsoft Graph API. Data updates in real time — refreshing the page will reflect the latest entries.
Who do I contact if something isn't working?
Raise it with your Newton Admin. For access issues, they can update user assignments directly via the Admin link on the Newton home screen.
Are survey responses confidential?
Your answers are confidential, not anonymous. Other survey respondents cannot see your responses. Only Newton Leadership, People Ops and Admins (and the SharePoint site owners) can open the survey data, and results in Newton are reported in aggregate. Responses are stored against a random ID rather than your name, but SharePoint records who created each item, so someone with that access could in principle link a response to its author. That is why we describe the survey as confidential rather than anonymous. A separate completion record notes that you have responded (so the widget clears from your homepage) but it contains no answers.
Can I edit my survey response after submitting?
No. Once submitted, responses are final. The survey widget will no longer appear on your homepage.
What are bench assignments and do I need to manage them manually?
Bench assignments are automatically generated records that represent periods when an employee has no active customer assignment. They are created and maintained using the ↻ Sync Bench button on the Assignments tab — you do not need to create or delete them manually. Syncing keeps the utilisation forecast accurate by filling gaps between customer assignments through to the end of the current year.
Who can see my Development & Performance records?
You, your line manager, your CSD, and Admin and People Ops. Leadership does not see them by default. Your line manager is the person above you on the Org Chart.
Why is My Development or Team Development missing from my menu?
The pages only appear when Development & Performance is switched on for you. While it is being piloted, that means the named pilot people plus their line managers and CSDs. Once it is On, it means everyone in the Delivery team plus their line managers and CSDs. My Development also needs your work email to be linked to your People record, and Team Development needs people to report to you in Newton. If it looks wrong, ask People Ops.
Can I change my prep after the meeting has happened?
You can keep editing your prep until your line manager marks the meeting as held. After that it is read-only. Goals and actions can still be updated afterwards from the Goals & actions tab, although the wording of anything agreed in a signed meeting is locked.
What happens to my records if my line manager changes?
Your new line manager and CSD see your whole history. Your previous line manager and CSD keep read-only access to the records from when they managed you.
Why haven't I had any PDP reminders?
Reminders only start once Development & Performance is On (people in the pilot get none), and never for meetings or actions due before the "Reminders from" date that an Admin sets. They appear as notifications on the Newton bell and never contain the content of your meetings.
Are Bi-Annual scores really blind?
Yes. You score yourself and your line manager scores you without seeing each other's scores. Nobody, including Admin and People Ops, sees either set until both sides have submitted and the scores are revealed.

User Roles

The People module is accessible to the roles below. Access level varies — Delivery Managers and Talent Partners have limited access (Scorecards, the Org Chart and, where Development & Performance applies to them, My Development and Team Development), and Sales & Marketing and Staff the Org Chart only.

Admin

Full access to all People module pages. Can add, edit, and delete employees and invoices; assignments are managed on the Project page. Sees all data across the entire workforce. In Development & Performance, Admins set the Off / Pilot / On switch and can see every record (Team Development is read-only for people they do not manage).

Leadership

Read-only access to all People module pages. Can view the dashboard, employee list, deployment timeline, and invoices. Cannot add or edit employees, assignments or invoices; can add Org Chart placeholders and edit non-leader reporting lines. Leadership does not see Development & Performance pages or records.

People Ops

For the People function. Full access to all People module pages, like Leadership, and can also add and edit employees and invoices, and build, run and close Engagement surveys. Can't edit Leadership reporting lines on the Org Chart (Admin only). In Development & Performance, People Ops run PDP Setup (framework, templates, cycle, calibration) and see every record, but cannot change the Off / Pilot / On switch.

Delivery Manager

Scorecards (for the Talent Partners on their projects) and the Org Chart, read-only. Delivery Managers land directly on Scorecards when they open the People module — other pages are not visible to this role, apart from My Development and Team Development when Development & Performance applies to them.

Talent Partner

Their own Scorecard and the Org Chart, read-only. Talent Partners land directly on Scorecards when they open the People module — other pages are not visible to this role, apart from My Development (and Team Development for anyone with people reporting to them) when Development & Performance applies to them.

Sales & Marketing · Staff

Org Chart only, read-only. They land on the Org Chart when they open the People module; other pages (including Development & Performance) are not visible to these roles.

Exporting to PDF

Pages with an ⎙ Export PDF button can be saved as a PDF document. Click the button to open your browser's print dialog, then configure it as follows before saving:

Destination

Set to Save as PDF. This saves the output as a file rather than sending it to a physical printer.

Options

Under the Options section, ensure Headers and footers is unticked and Background graphics is ticked. Background graphics must be enabled for the branded header block to print correctly.

These settings are not saved by the browser between sessions — you will need to set them each time you export.

People Dashboard

Admin Leadership People Ops

A real-time overview of workforce performance, utilisation, and revenue. The KPI strip is always fixed to current year YTD — the period filters below it control the three detail panels only.

KPI Strip

Four tiles showing company-wide metrics as of today:

Estimated Revenue — total billed revenue for the current year to date.

Utilisation — billed person-days as a percentage of assigned person-days, YTD, with each person counted once per day. Flagged below 85%: amber 75–85%, red below 75%.

Active Customers — number of distinct customers with a current active assignment today. The delta indicator (green/red) shows the change vs the last day of the previous quarter.

Billed Headcount — number of employees on a billed assignment today. The delta indicator shows the change vs the last day of the previous quarter.

Team Utilisation Line Graph

A full-width line graph showing monthly utilisation across the current year. The solid line covers January through to the current month (actual data). The dotted line continues from the current month to December, calculated from assignments already scheduled in the system (forecast). Hover over any data point to see the exact percentage for that month.

Period Filters

Use the Year, Month, and Quarter dropdowns to filter the three panels below. Selecting a month clears the quarter selection and vice versa. The year filter applies to both.

Panel 1 — Team Utilisation

A summary table showing utilisation percentage and headcount (utilised / total active) per role band — SDM, STP, TP, PTP. A totals row shows the overall blended utilisation. Below the table, a monthly trend bar chart shows how utilisation has moved across the selected period.

Panel 2 — Estimated Revenue

Two side-by-side tables for the selected period: revenue by customer (sorted highest to lowest, excluding Unassigned/Internal) and revenue by project type (Embedded, CoE, Transformation, LCI).

Panel 3 — Workforce Segmentation

Three headcount breakdowns of currently active employees (not period-filtered): by location, by contract type, and by role band. Each shows count and percentage of total.

Upcoming End Dates

Lists any active employees with an end date in the next 60 days, sorted by nearest first.

The Days Remaining cell is flagged: ⚠ within 30 days, ! within 31–60 days.

Org Chart

Admin Leadership People Ops Sales & Marketing Delivery Manager Talent Partner Staff

A line-and-box chart of how Momentum is organised, built from the People list, Assignments and Leadership access. Everyone who can open the People module can read it. It shows current assignments only, so it always reflects today. People who have been added but have not started yet are not shown; they appear on their start date.

Click a leader, CSD or SDM who has people beneath them to collapse their part of the chart, and click again to open it. A collapsed box shows how many people are in that team, counting each person once even if they work on several projects, and leaving out "To be hired" placeholders. Expand all and Collapse all at the top open or close every box at once. Export PDF prints the chart as you see it, so collapse the parts you don't need first.

Reading the chart

The chart runs top-down: Leadership → CSD → project → the people on that project. Project boxes are colour coded by project type, as on the Deployment Timeline. Internal staff (Marketing, Sales, Internal TA, Operations and People) sit under the leader they report to, grouped into a box per function, and show their function instead of a level.

Below the chart, up to three panels collect anyone or anything that does not fit the tree:

  • Unassigned projects — projects with no CSD owner yet.
  • Internal — no reporting line — internal staff who have not been given a leader.
  • Unassigned — the bench: deployable people with no current customer assignment (or only a bench one). CSDs, internal staff, partners and placeholders never appear here.

To be hired

A vacancy is shown as a dashed box labelled To be hired in place of a location. Placeholders appear on the chart only — they do not count towards utilisation, the bench or the Deployment Timeline.

Exporting

Use ⎚ Export PDF to print the chart. It is scaled to fit one landscape A4 page and titled with the current month and year.

Placeholders and reporting lines

Admin, Leadership and People Ops see two extra buttons. Delivery Managers, Talent Partners, Sales & Marketing and Staff see the chart read-only.

  • + Placeholders — add, edit or remove a "To be hired" box. Give it a name, a level (an SDM sits directly under the team box; STP and TP report into the SDM) and a team. Pick an existing project, or choose new team, give it a name and choose its CSD owner.
  • Edit reporting lines — set the CSD owner of each project (LCI and Transformation projects are listed like any other), and who each CSD and internal person reports to. Changes save together with Save changes.
Leadership “Reports to” rows in Edit reporting lines can only be changed by an Admin. Leadership and People Ops can still edit everything else on that screen.

Employee Tracker

Admin Leadership People Ops

A two-tab view of the workforce — employees and their assignments. Use the tab bar at the top to switch between the two views.

Employees tab

Lists all active employees sorted by role band then name, showing level, contract type, location, start date, end date, and status. Toggle Show inactive employees to include leavers in the list.

Admins can add new employees using + Add Employee and edit existing records via the Edit link on each row.

Staff type is Deployable (the default), Internal or External. Internal is for payroll staff in an internal function: Marketing, Sales, Internal TA, Operations or People. An internal employee has a Function instead of a Level and shows as "Internal · Function" in the Level column. They are never counted in utilisation, bench or deployable headcount, and can't be picked in the assignment form. They do appear on payroll, and on the Org Chart under the leader they report to (set this in Edit reporting lines).

External is for a partner from outside Momentum who works on a client project. They have no Level, Contract Type or Salary, and show as "Partner". Add them to a project from its Project Team: they then show under that project on the Org Chart and on the Deployment Timeline. Their billing counts towards revenue, but they are never counted in utilisation, headcount, the bench, workforce segmentation, payroll or survey numbers.

A deployable employee's Level field (CSD, SDM, STP, TP, PTP) determines which role band they appear in across all dashboard calculations. Keep this accurate when editing records.

Assignments tab

Lists assignment records showing employee name, level, customer, project type, start and end dates, monthly bill rate, and billed status. Defaults to current assignments.

Use the Status filter to switch between Current, Former, Planned, or All assignments. Filter further by Customer or Project Type using the dropdowns.

This tab is read-only. Project assignments are added, edited and removed on the Project page. Click a customer in the list to open its Project page, then use its Project Team section: it links the person to the project and keeps their bench records up to date.

Auto-generated bench records and old assignments for customers that have no Newton project are shown as plain text and can't be changed here. Bench records are managed by ↻ Sync Bench; a correction to an old assignment is made in the SharePoint Assignments list.

The Billed field on an assignment determines whether it contributes to revenue and utilisation calculations. An assignment marked Billed = No only lowers utilisation on days when that person has no billed assignment — an unbilled assignment running alongside a billed one does not count against them.

Adding an SDM to an Exec Search or MG AI engagement: on that project's Project Team, add the SDM and set Billed = No. The retainer and placement fee fields are hidden and nothing is recorded against the SDM, so revenue is unaffected (the Rate column shows "Unbilled"). The SDM then appears under that project on the Org Chart, with the Talent Partner reporting into them, and as a bar on the Deployment Timeline.

Bench Sync

The ↻ Sync Bench button (visible to Admins) automatically generates and maintains bench assignments for all active billable employees. When run, it identifies gaps between a person's customer assignments for the rest of the current year — from today up to their employment end date (or 31 December if no end date is set) — and creates an Unassigned / Internal assignment to fill each gap.

Bench records are marked as auto-generated and are managed entirely by the sync. If you add or extend a customer assignment that covers a period previously flagged as bench, running sync again will remove the now-redundant bench record automatically.

Saving or removing someone on a Project Team re-syncs that person's bench records automatically; use ↻ Sync Bench to rebuild them for everyone. The timestamp next to the button shows when the last sync ran. Bench assignments should not be edited manually — let the sync manage them.

Supplier Invoices

Admin Leadership People Ops

Tracks invoices issued through different supplier arrangements. Each invoice is logged with its number, dates, amount, notes, and payment status.

Summary bar

At the top of the page, three figures give an at-a-glance view of the invoice position: total outstanding amount, number of overdue invoices, and the date of the oldest overdue invoice.

Invoice status

Each invoice has one of three statuses: Sent (issued, awaiting payment), Overdue (Sent with a due date in the past), or Paid. Overdue status is calculated automatically — no manual flagging is needed.

Adding and editing invoices

Admins can add new invoices using + Add Invoice and edit existing records via the Edit link on each row. Once payment is received, use Mark Paid on the row to update the status directly without opening the edit form.

Supplier Invoice amounts are tracked separately from the Estimated Revenue figures on the People Dashboard. The dashboard calculates revenue from assignment bill rates — invoice amounts here reflect actual G-P charges which may differ.

Scorecards

Admin Leadership People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

The Scorecards page provides activity analytics and performance benchmarking for each Talent Partner. Delivery Managers and Talent Partners can open it, plus the Org Chart, but no other People module page. Delivery Managers see the Talent Partners on their projects; a Talent Partner sees only their own card.

What the scorecards show

Each Talent Partner is shown as a card displaying a rolling quarterly view of their pipeline activity and funnel conversion metrics across all roles. This gives a consistent, comparable picture of performance — how much activity is being generated, and how effectively it is converting through the funnel.

Benchmarking

Conversion rates are compared against learned benchmarks — what comparable roles actually achieve, learned from the last 52 weeks of Weekly Activity. Each Talent Partner's benchmark is weighted by the functions and locations they work in, so a TP on hard markets is compared with those markets, and their own activity is left out so they are never benchmarked against themselves. Where there is little data, the benchmark leans toward Newton's company targets (CONFIG.ANALYTICS_BENCHMARKS), and it never drops below 80% of the target, so a weak market can't hide a real gap. A learned benchmark can also sit above target — in a strong market, hitting the target may still show amber. Hover over a rate to see the benchmark used. Contact your Newton Admin if the targets or learning settings (CONFIG.LEARNED_BENCHMARKS) need adjusting.

Hires counts the placements logged under the Talent Partner's name in the last 13 weeks (by Offer Accepted date) — the same figure the Placements table gives for them. Offer success is worked out from the offers and hires Talent Partners record in Weekly Activity, so on one card the two can differ.

Scorecards data is calculated from Weekly Activity records, except Hires and Average time to hire, which come from Placements. Ensure activity is being logged consistently each week for the funnel metrics to be accurate.

Internal TA staff, who log Momentum's own hiring on the Momentum Global (Internal) project, get a card built from that work. It is compared against client benchmarks, and internal activity never feeds anyone else's benchmark.

Engagement

Admin Leadership People Ops

The Engagement page is where Admins and People Ops build and manage quarterly pulse surveys, and where Admins, Leadership and People Ops view the results. Survey responses are confidential — see the FAQ for details.

Survey Template

Before a survey can be run, a template must be created. Click Manage Template to open the template editor. Set a title, an introduction paragraph (shown to respondents before the first question), the target audience, and an optional target activation date.

Once the template is saved, add questions using + Add Question. Each question has a type — Rating (1–5 scale with optional labels), Single Choice, Multiple Choice, or Free Text. Questions can be reordered using the up/down arrows and deleted individually. At least 3 questions are required before a run can be activated.

The template title can be updated before each quarter's run to reflect the current period (e.g. "Q3 2026 Pulse Survey"). Updating the title does not affect historical run results — past runs retain their own labels.

Activating a Run

Click Activate Run to start a new survey run. Set the run label (e.g. "Q3 2026"), confirm the open and close dates, and click Activate Now. The survey widget will appear on the homepage immediately for Talent Partners, Delivery Managers, People Ops, Sales & Marketing and Staff users. The eligible respondent count shown is every active employee on the Employee Tracker except CSDs.

Only one run can be active at a time. If a run is already active, you must close it before activating a new one.

Viewing Results

Once responses start coming in, the Engagement page displays a summary dashboard for the active run. Each question is shown with its response distribution — rating questions show average score and a bar chart of responses, choice questions show percentage breakdowns, and free-text responses are listed in full.

Use the run selector dropdown to switch between historical runs and compare results over time. The Trends tab shows how average scores have changed across runs for each rating question.

Closing a Run

Click Close Run to end the active survey. Respondents will no longer be able to submit. The run's results remain visible in the dashboard indefinitely under the run selector.

Exporting Results

Click Export PDF to save the current run's results as a PDF. This uses the browser print dialog — set the destination to Save as PDF and enable Background graphics before saving. See the Exporting to PDF section for full instructions.

The survey widget remains on a respondent's homepage until they submit. If they close the browser and return later, the widget will still be there — responses are tracked in SharePoint, not the browser.

Development & Performance: how it works

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

Development & Performance (PDP) is where Newton keeps the four development conversations between a person and their line manager: the meeting notes, the goals agreed and the actions that follow. It starts with the Delivery team (levels TP, STP, PTP and SDM). CSDs take part as reviewers.

The four meetings

Annual Development & Goal Setting in January. Bi-Annual Performance & Progression Review in July. Quarterly Review in April and October. Monthly Development Check-In in every other month. That makes twelve meetings a year. Each one is due on the last day of its month.

Newton tracks what is due, due soon and overdue. It does not send calendar invites: line managers book the meetings in Outlook themselves.

Who sees what

Each person's records are visible to the person, their line manager, their CSD, and Admin and People Ops. Leadership does not see them by default. The employee can complete their own prep, track their goals and actions, and sign off each meeting.

Off, Pilot and On

An Admin controls how far PDP reaches. Off: nobody sees it except Admin and People Ops, who can preview it. Pilot: only the named pilot people and their line managers and CSDs see it, and nobody is sent reminders. On: everyone in scope and their reviewers see it, and reminders start. Someone removed from the pilot keeps seeing their existing records. Switching to Off hides PDP from everyone except Admin and People Ops, even people who already have records.

Joiners, existing staff and promotions

A new joiner's first meeting is an Annual-format goal setting, due 30 days after they start. Any calendar meeting due within three weeks after that is skipped. The July Bi-Annual is only scored if they started on or before 31 March.

Someone who already works at Momentum and is brought into PDP part-way through the year follows the normal calendar. Meetings whose month has already passed are created as Skipped with the reason "Before PDP start".

If someone is promoted, their Bi-Annual is scored against the new level once they have been in it for three months.

Admin and People Ops can preview PDP even when it is Off or when they are not in the pilot. A preview is read-only: nothing can be saved or signed.

My Development

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

Your own page: your year of meetings, your prep, the agreed record of each meeting, and your goals and actions. It appears in the People menu once PDP is live for you and your work email is linked to your People record.

My year

The top of the page shows your name, level, line manager and CSD, with counts of meetings done, due soon and overdue, and a Year selector. Under it, My year shows one card per meeting with its due date and a status: Upcoming, Due soon, Overdue, Done or Skipped (with the reason). A line on the card says where it is up to, for example "Prep open", "Prep ready", "Held · your sign-off is needed" or "Signed off". Click a card to open the meeting.

Preparing

The Annual, Bi-Annual and Quarterly meetings have prep questions for you to answer beforehand. Prep opens on the first day of the month the meeting is due. For a Quarterly it opens two days before the meeting date your line manager sets, and for a joiner's goal setting it opens 30 days before it is due. Use Save draft as you go and Mark prep ready when you are done. Your answers are private to you, your line manager and your CSD, and you can keep editing until the meeting is marked as held.

The Monthly Development Check-In needs no prep. The meeting shows "No prep needed for this meeting" and has no prep buttons. The Quarterly meeting has a question about performance against KPIs: answer it in your own words, and use Open my Scorecard to see your numbers where you have a Scorecard.

After the meeting

Once your line manager marks the meeting as held, you can read the agreed record: the notes, the goals and the actions, plus your manager's prep. Click Sign off this record to sign. Your line manager signs as well, and the meeting becomes Signed off once you have both signed.

The Goals & actions tab beside My year shows your goal tracker (see Goals & actions below).

Team Development

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

For line managers and CSDs. The page appears when people report to you in Newton (a CSD sees everyone beneath them) and PDP is live for you. Admin and People Ops always see it.

The team table

The table lists each person, their line manager, their next meeting and what needs attention, plus how many meetings they have done. Counts across the top show how many meetings are overdue, due soon, prep ready, waiting for a signature, and how many people have no meetings yet. People with something overdue or waiting for you are listed first.

Use View to switch between Whole tree, Direct reports and All staff. All staff is for Admin and People Ops and is read-only except for the people they manage themselves. Use Year to look at other years. For a Quarterly meeting you can set the meeting date straight from the table.

One person's year

Click a name to see that person's year. The Meetings tab lists their meetings and the Goals & actions tab shows their tracker.

Running a meeting

Open a meeting to see the employee's prep, and the commitments from the previous held meeting that are still open, so nothing is dropped. Write your own prep and the discussion notes, record the goals and actions agreed, and set the meeting date. If something is missing, a box titled Before you can mark this as held lists it. When you are ready, click Mark as held, then Sign off as manager.

You can keep editing a held meeting until the first signature (yours or the employee's). After that the record is locked.

What you can change depends on whether you are named on that person's record, not on your Newton role. Only the person's own line manager or CSD can run their meeting, even though CSDs have the Admin role. Anyone else sees the record read-only.

Goals & actions

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

The Goals & actions tab appears on My Development and on a person's page in Team Development. It is one live view of the year's goals and actions, and you can update it between meetings without opening a meeting.

Goals

Goals cascade: Annual goals, then H2 goals for the second half of the year, then Quarterly goals. A goal can be linked to a parent goal above it. Each goal has a category (Business, Development, Behaviour or Career), and can record why it matters, how success will be measured, the support needed and a target date.

Every goal has a RAG (Red, Amber or Green) and a status (Open, Achieved or Dropped). A goal's rolled-up RAG shows the worst RAG among its open goals beneath it, so you can see where the pressure is without opening each one. There is a limit on how many open goals one person can hold at each level in a year.

Actions

Each action has an owner (the employee or the manager) and a due date, and is ticked off when it is done. How many actions a meeting can record is set in the meeting template.

Who can edit

The employee, their line manager and their CSD can add and edit goals and actions. Once a meeting is signed, the wording of the goals and actions agreed in it is locked. RAG, status and the done tick can always be changed. A goal you no longer want is marked as Dropped rather than deleted. When the year's cycle is closed, everything is read-only.

Bi-Annual scoring

Admin People Ops Delivery Manager Talent Partner

The July meeting includes a scoring block. The employee and the line manager each score six pillars from 1 to 3 against the descriptors for the employee's level. There is no reward or salary recommendation in this review.

Step by step

  • Employee self-score. In My Development, open the Bi-Annual and score each pillar with your evidence. Use Save scores as draft until you are ready to submit.
  • Manager score. The line manager (or CSD) scores the same pillars from Team Development, with a rationale for each. Scoring is blind: neither side can see the other's scores yet.
  • Reveal. Once both sides have submitted, Newton reveals each side's scores to the other. This takes a few minutes and the block shows it is unlocking. Until then nobody else, including Admin and People Ops, can see either set.
  • Agreeing scores in the meeting. The manager records an agreed score per pillar. If the two scores are the same, that is the agreed score. If they are one point apart, the manager decides: the agreed score must be one of the two, with a reason. If they are two points apart, the pillar goes to the calibration panel.
  • Overall score and band. The overall score is the average of the six pillars to one decimal place: 1.0 to 1.5 is Below, 1.6 to 2.4 is Meets and 2.5 to 3.0 is Exceeding. A total of exactly 1.5 or 2.5 sits on a boundary: the manager agrees which of the two bands it belongs in, or sends it to the panel.
  • Progression readiness. The manager chooses Ready now, Ready in 6–12 months or Not yet ready, with a note.
  • Manager track (Level 4 only). Newton suggests a manager-track flag when the agreed score is 3 on Support, Curious and Adapt. The manager confirms it, with a reason, or leaves it.

A Bi-Annual can only be marked as held once both sides have submitted, the scores are revealed, every pillar is agreed or sent to the panel, any boundary total is settled and readiness is chosen. Signing off is allowed while a pillar is still with the panel.

Scoring for the PTP level stays switched off until the descriptors for that level have been written in PDP Setup. Until then the scoring block shows that there are no descriptors for the level.

Calibration

Admin People Ops

After the Bi-Annuals are held, Admin and People Ops review the results together on the Calibration tab of PDP Setup, so that ratings are consistent across the team.

The panel

Choose the year to see every held Bi-Annual. The distribution strip compares the share of Below, Meets and Exceeding results with the target range for each. It is a warning only and never stops you locking ratings. Each person is a case with a state: needs panel, ready, locked or released.

The cases that need the panel are those with an escalated pillar or boundary total, and anyone Below or Exceeding overall. For these, the panel sets a calibrated score per pillar and writes a reason wherever it differs from the agreed score. Use Lock all non-panel cases to lock the rest in one go. You cannot calibrate your own review: someone else on the panel does it.

Locking and releasing

Locking is per person and makes the ratings final. Only an Admin can unlock a person, and must give a reason, which is logged. After locking, the person's line manager or CSD clicks Release to employee on the meeting. The employee then sees their overall score and band, the calibrated score for each pillar and pointers for where to develop next. When PDP is On they also get a notification.

PDP Setup

Admin People Ops

The behind-the-scenes pages for Development & Performance. PDP Setup is in the People menu and has four tabs.

Framework

The capability framework: the descriptor for each level, track (IC or MG), pillar and band. Edit a descriptor in place and Newton records who changed it and when. It starts with Levels 1 to 5, with Level 5 on the IC track empty until the PTP framework is ready.

Templates

The four meeting templates. Each is made of sections, and each section of blocks of six fixed types: prep question, discussion prompt, goal setting, goal review, actions and scoring. You can change the wording, the order and which blocks a template has, but not invent new types. A published template is never edited: save a new draft and publish it as the next version. A meeting that has started keeps the version it started with. Seed defaults fills an empty setup with the starting framework and templates.

Cycle

Creates each person's year of meetings. Choose the year (from November the next year is offered too) and click Preview, which writes nothing, to see what will be created, what cannot be generated (for example no work email or line manager) and who is waiting. Click Apply to create it. It is safe to run again: it only adds or fixes what is missing, and skips meetings for people who have left. A banner on PDP Setup warns when people are missing meetings. The tab also has a Tidy goal periods tool for older goals.

Each person needs a line manager and a work email on their People record. Line managers are set on the Org Chart under Edit reporting lines (Line manager — delivery staff). A promotion date goes in the Level since field on the Add / Edit Employee form.

Calibration

See Calibration above.

The switch, reminders and the dashboard tile

Admins set Off, Pilot or On from the PDP tab of the Config Panel, where they also pick the pilot people and set the Reminders from date. Reminders are bell notifications for prep due soon, meetings overdue and actions due soon, and only go out while PDP is On. Admin and People Ops also see a PDP completion tile on the People Dashboard, with counts only.

Set the Reminders from date before switching PDP to On, so people are not reminded about meetings that were due before go-live.

Homepage Admin

Admin

Admins can control two homepage features from the Homepage tab in the Newton OS Admin panel (accessible via the Admin link on the Newton home screen).

Announcement Banner

A scrolling ticker that appears at the bottom of the Newton home screen, visible to all users. Use it to share team-wide messages, reminders, or announcements.

To set a message, type it into the Message field and click Save. The banner appears on the home screen immediately for all users. To remove it, click Clear Banner — the ticker disappears until a new message is saved.

The message is stored in SharePoint and shown to every user on page load — it is not browser-specific. One active message can be set at a time.

Seasonal Effects

A canvas animation that plays on the Newton home screen. One effect can be active at a time. Toggle any effect on using its button — clicking again turns it off. Changes take effect immediately for all users on their next page load.

Available effects: Spring, Summer Scene, Autumn, Snowfall, and Christmas Lights.

Seasonal effects were previously per-browser (localStorage). They are now stored in SharePoint, meaning the Admin controls the effect for all users from a single setting.
